**RECOMMENDATIONS REPORT**

**Zomato Bangalore Business Analytics**

**Executive Summary**

Based on the analysis of the 51,205 listings in the Zomato Bangalore dataset, the market is heavily dominated by affordable, high-volume dining options centered in key commercial and academic hubs.

* Market Composition & Core Segment: Quick Bites (37.9% / 19,386 listings) and Delivery (50.2% / 25,695 listings) represent the vast majority of platform listings. The average cost for two across all listings is ₹555.17, with a median of ₹400.00, indicating that price accessibility is a key driver of platform supply.  
* Geographic Concentration: Supply is densely concentrated in high-density, multi-demographic neighborhoods, led by BTM Layout (5,078 location listings / 3,252 city listings).  
* Feature Adoption & Performance:  
  * Online Ordering: Adopted by 59.2% of listings.  
  * Table Booking: Restricted to premium/dining-out segments, with an overall adoption rate of 12.5%.  
  * Overall Rating Standard: The platform average rating is 2.99 / 5.00 across all listings. However, among active listings with substantial review volume, ratings cluster tightly between 3.60 (50th percentile) and 3.90 (75th percentile).  
* Cuisine Diversity: While North Indian is the single most common cuisine (2,865 listings), restaurants offer an average of 2 cuisines, reflecting strong consumer demand for multi-cuisine menus.


**Strategic Business Recommendations** 

1. Optimize Online Ordering Infrastructure in High-Density Hubs:  
   Insight*:* Over 59% of listings support online ordering, and delivery accounts for 25,697 listings. Locations like BTM and Koramangala host thousands of listings.  
   Action*:* Focus logistics network optimization and delivery-partner allocation around high-density zones like BTM. Create targeted onboarding packages for the remaining 40.8% of merchants without online ordering to drive platform GMV.  
     
2. Monetize and Expand Table Booking for Premium Segments:  
   Insight*:* Table booking adoption is low at 12.5% platform-wide, yet higher-priced Casual Dining, Cafes, and Buffets command higher cost-for-two metrics (up to ₹2,000).  
   Action*:* Drive targeted merchant campaigns to pitch table-reservation software to Casual Dining and Pub/Bar categories, pairing reservation capabilities with exclusive dine-in discounts.

 

3. Promote Multi-Cuisine Cloud Kitchen Concepts:  
   Insight*:* The average place offers 2 cuisines, and top single cuisines like North Indian dominate.  
   Action*:* Encourage cloud kitchen partners in high-demand areas to offer multi-cuisine menus (combining North Indian, Chinese, and Fast Food) to capture broader basket sizes per order.

4. Merchant Quality & Rating Improvement Program:  
   Insight*:* The median review count is 41 reviews per listing, with 25% of listings falling at or below a 3.0 rating.  
   Action*:* Implement automated feedback and review-generation prompts for low-review listings (below the 50th percentile of 41 reviews) to help emerging restaurants build social proof and move toward the 3.6–3.9 quality benchmark.
